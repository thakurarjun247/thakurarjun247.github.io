Junior AI Associate

I deliver AI training and consulting to corporate teams. Behind every client call and workshop there's research, outlines, slides, and demos to prep — and I need a sharp, technical junior to take that off my plate. You'll learn AI fast, work directly with me, and grow into building real AI applications.

Apply NowSee the Role
Why this role exists

Sales and marketing are covered — but that side isn't technical. I need someone who speaks the language: research a client before the call, turn a brief into a training outline, build the labs, and take call notes that actually capture the requirements. It's a real learning seat: you learn practical AI by shipping client work, not by watching tutorials.

The Role

The technical right hand: client research, training prep, call support — and, over time, building AI apps.

Dewas preferred, Indore OKFull-time or 4 hrs/dayPaid from day one1-month probation

What you'll do

  • Research each client before calls — their industry, teams, and where AI fits
  • Draft custom training outlines with ChatGPT and Claude, from my templates
  • Build training material — slides, hands-on labs, sample datasets, demo notebooks
  • Sit in on client calls, capture requirements, draft follow-ups and proposals
  • Answer basic technical questions for the sales team
  • Over time: build demos and small AI apps — RAG chatbots, simple agents — with Claude Code
You prep and build · I deliver

What we're looking for

  • Sharp and fast — you figure things out without being walked through every step
  • You use ChatGPT or Claude daily and know how to get good output from them
  • Clear written English — outlines and emails that need little editing
  • Basic Python, or the drive to get there quickly
  • Can explain a technical idea simply to a non-technical person
  • Reliable — you hit deadlines and show up
Speed of learning wins here

Nice to have (not required)

  • You've built something with an LLM API — even a toy project
  • You know terms like LLM, RAG, embeddings, agents, and prompt engineering
  • Comfortable in PowerPoint or Google Slides
  • CS, IT, or AI/ML branch — final year or recent grad
Projects beat pedigree

What you won't need

  • Prior job experience
  • Deep ML or math knowledge — I'll teach you what matters
  • A big-name college
  • To lead client calls on day one — you'll grow into it
I teach the rest

Where This Role Goes

A rough path — how fast you move depends on you.

First 3 months

Research and prep: client briefs, training outlines, slides, and labs. Sit in on calls and learn how consulting actually works.

Months 3–6

Own training material end to end, build demos for workshops, and handle technical questions from leads.

Month 6 onward

Build AI applications for clients with Claude, and take on parts of client conversations yourself.

How to Apply

The task below tells me more than any resume — please don't skip it.

1. Email me

Send an email to arjun@arjunthakur.dev with the subject line "Application: Junior AI Associate". Say whether you want full-time or part-time, and where you're based.

2. Introduce yourself

In 3–4 lines: who you are, what you're studying (or studied), and anything you've built. Add a GitHub link if you have one.

3. Do the 1-hour task

Using ChatGPT or Claude, create a 1-day training outline for "Generative AI for HR teams". Then explain RAG to a non-technical client in 5 lines or fewer. Paste both into your email — this is the part I read first.

4. Optional bonus

A short Python script that calls any LLM API, shared as a GitHub link or gist. Optional, but it stands out.

Sharp, technical, and hungry to learn? Send your application with the task — shortlisted candidates get a short call.

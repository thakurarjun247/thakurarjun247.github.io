Program summary

Referral partner · Companies & agencies · Worldwide · Commission on revenue

We are onboarding companies and agencies that can find us clients for AI consulting. When a client comes to us through your reference, you earn an agreed percentage of the revenue we make from that client.

About us

We are an AI engineering and consulting practice led by Arjun Thakur, Principal AI Engineer (ex-Amazon, Agoda). We help businesses plan, build and ship AI: see our AI consulting services for what your referred clients will get.

Who we are looking for

  • Lead generation and business development agencies.
  • Consultancies, software agencies and digital agencies whose clients ask for AI work.
  • Companies with their own network of clients.
  • Partners in any country who can introduce decision-makers that need AI consulting.

What you can refer

  • AI strategy and AI adoption consulting.
  • Agentic AI, RAG and LLM application development.
  • AI architecture reviews and cost optimisation.
  • Fractional CTO and technical advisory engagements.

How it works

  1. Register: fill in the partner form with your details and the commission you expect.
  2. Agree terms: we review your details, get on a short call and agree the percentage in writing.
  3. Introduce clients: you refer a client; we handle the scoping, proposal and delivery.
  4. Get paid: when the client pays us, you receive your agreed share of that revenue.

Commission and payment terms

  • Revenue share: you earn an agreed percentage of the revenue we receive from each client that came through your reference.
  • Your choice of model: propose a fixed percentage for every referred client, or decide it project by project. Tell us which you prefer in the form.
  • Paid on revenue received: your share is calculated on the amount the referred client actually pays us.
  • Agreed in writing: the percentage and payment terms are confirmed before you start introducing clients.

No upfront payment

We do not pay anything upfront: no retainer, no advance, no sign-up fee and no fee per lead. We pay only from the amount we actually receive from the clients and projects you refer. If a referred client does not sign or does not pay, no commission is due.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pay referral partners anything upfront?

No. There is no upfront payment, retainer, advance or fee for leads. Commission is paid only from the revenue we actually receive from a client that came through your reference.

How much commission do referral partners earn?

An agreed percentage of the revenue we receive from each client you refer. You can propose a fixed percentage that applies to every referred client, or we can decide it project by project. The percentage is agreed in writing before you start introducing clients.

When is the commission paid?

After the referred client pays us. Your share is calculated on the amount we receive for that client's project.

Who can become a referral partner?

Companies and agencies that can introduce clients who need AI consulting: lead generation and business development agencies, consultancies, software and digital agencies, and firms with their own client network.

Technical Consultant, Software & AI Engineering

Remote (India) · Full-time · 3 PM – 12 AM IST · 2+ years' experience

About the role

This is a role with Arjun Thakur's practice, working with a staffing firm in the San Francisco Bay Area as its technical consultant. Talk to hiring companies about the engineers they need, from traditional full-stack and backend development to AI engineering, and help design upskilling programs for engineers aiming for high-paying software and AI engineering jobs.

Responsibilities

  • Talk to hiring companies (the firm's clients) about their staffing requirements, on calls and in writing
  • Cover the full range: traditional full-stack and backend development, AI engineering, agentic AI, RAG, evals, and LLMs
  • Turn each requirement into a clear role profile: skills, seniority, tech stack, and what to test for
  • Help design upskilling programs for engineers who want high-paying software engineering and AI engineering jobs: curriculum, hands-on projects, and interview prep
  • Stay current on AI engineering so client conversations stay credible

Requirements

  • 2+ years of professional experience as a full-stack or backend software engineer, or an AI/ML engineer
  • Excellent English, both spoken and written
  • Strong communication skills: comfortable talking to US clients about their hiring needs
  • Working knowledge of LLMs, RAG, agentic AI, and evals
  • Solid grounding in full-stack and backend development: APIs, databases, cloud, and system design
  • Able to explain technical trade-offs clearly to engineers and non-technical people alike
  • Self-driven: you work independently, without hand-holding

Nice to have

  • Built or shipped an LLM-powered application (RAG, agents)
  • Experience interviewing or hiring engineers
  • Experience mentoring, teaching, or creating technical content
  • Worked with US clients or US teams

Location & hours

  • Remote, from anywhere in India
  • Full-time, 3 PM – 12 AM IST (overlaps with the US Pacific morning)
  • Start: immediate

Pay

  • ₹40,000 / month

How to apply

  1. Email hello@arjunthakur.dev with this exact subject line: Technical Consultant | Your Name
  2. In the email, write 3–4 lines about yourself: current role, years of experience, and main stack. Attach your resume and add your LinkedIn profile.
  3. We'll email a short task to shortlisted candidates.

Please don't call or WhatsApp. Applications are accepted by email only. Emails without the subject line above may be missed.

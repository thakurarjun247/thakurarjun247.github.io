The track isn't live yet. I'm actively prepping for NVIDIA DLI certification (Fundamentals of Accelerated Computing with Modern CUDA C++, plus multi-GPU), and building the curriculum as I go.

Why CUDA, why now:

Everyone's shipping AI on top of GPUs. Very few engineers can actually write and optimize what runs on them.

Calling a framework is not the same as writing a fast kernel. The people who understand the GPU execution model, memory coalescing, occupancy, and multi-GPU scaling are scarce — and that gap is only widening as AI and HPC workloads grow.

So I'm aiming this at the hard part: real CUDA C++, from the execution model up through profiling and multi-GPU — not "import a library and hope."

What I'm testing:

Is there appetite for hands-on CUDA / GPU-programming training — corporate teams or 1:1?

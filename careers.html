Sales & Outreach Associate — Part-Time

The person who fills the top of the funnel: research, outreach, follow-up, and booked calls.

Indore or Dewas~2 hrs/day, flexibleStudents welcomeStipend + bonus

What you'll do

  • Research and build lists of the right people to reach — companies, L&D leaders, founders
  • Send outreach and — the part most people skip — follow up, consistently
  • Book qualified intro calls on my calendar
  • Write professional, warm emails and messages to senior people
  • Keep the pipeline and CRM clean and up to date
  • Light coordination — scheduling, reminders, formatting proposals
You set up conversations · I close

What we're looking for

  • Excellent spoken and written English — you can email a director without a typo and sound human, not robotic
  • Confident, personable communication — comfortable on calls and follow-ups
  • Reliable and organized — you actually send the fourth follow-up
  • Coachable — take a script and a CRM and run with it
  • Based in Indore or Dewas, available around 2 hrs a day
Communication & consistency win here

Nice to have (not required)

  • Studying or graduated in business, marketing, sales, or communications — BBA/MBA a plus
  • Any customer-facing, sales, BD, or tele-calling experience
  • Basic comfort with everyday tools — email, spreadsheets, LinkedIn, calendars
Attitude beats pedigree

What you won't need

  • Any technical or AI background — that's my job, not yours
  • A fancy degree or years of experience
  • To ever cold-close a deal — you set up the conversation, I take it from there

How to Apply

The task below tells me more than any resume — please don't skip it.

1. Email me

Send an email to arjun@arjunthakur.dev with the subject line "Application: Sales & Outreach Associate".

2. Introduce yourself

In 3–4 lines: who you are, where you're based (Indore or Dewas), and why this role suits you.

3. Do the 2-minute task

Write the follow-up email you'd send to a busy director who showed interest and then went quiet — and paste it into your email. This is the part I actually read first.

4. Optional bonus

Attach a 60-second voice note pitching a quick call, so I can hear how you communicate. Optional, but it stands out.
